Fair Eight

The block puzzle that plays fair.

No ads. No tricks. Eight rows, eight columns, and pieces anyone can check.

Checkable deals

Every game has a replay code. Anyone can re-run it and see the exact same pieces.

Move coach

After each game, Coach grades every placement and shows the move that cost you.

Daily puzzle

Same pieces for everyone, one try a day. Compare scores with friends.
