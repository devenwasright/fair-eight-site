Fair Eight privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Fair Eight is a block puzzle game for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Fair Eight has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, game progress, Daily results and settings (look, sound, vibration) are saved only in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

If you tap Share result, your phone's share sheet opens with a short text summary of your Daily game. You choose where it goes. The app doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

Fair Eight is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds online features, such as leaderboards, this page will be updated before that version is released, and it will say exactly what is shared.
